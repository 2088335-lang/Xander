About Us

Who We Are

Welcome to Xander The Explorer - Smart Transit System! We are students who created this website to help commuters in Cagayan de Oro City find public transportation routes more easily.

What We Do

Our website aims to provide useful information about public transportation routes, starting points, destinations, estimated fares, distances, and roads passed. It helps commuters become more familiar with routes and plan their trips more conveniently.

Our Mission

To provide accessible and easy-to-understand public transportation information for commuters in Cagayan de Oro City.

Our Vision

To become a helpful guide for commuters by making public transportation information easier to find and understand. We want to help people travel around Cagayan de Oro City with greater convenience and confidence.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to help students, workers, residents, and visitors find suitable public transportation routes. Through this website, we aim to make commuting simpler and help users plan their journeys.
